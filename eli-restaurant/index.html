Our Heritage & Hospitality

Three Culinary Traditions, One Table in London.

Tucked along picturesque 6-8 Bute Street in South Kensington, Eli’s Restaurant was born from a shared family passion: uniting the sunlit olive groves of the Mediterranean, the slow-turning rotisseries of Southern France, and the centuries-old art of traditional Algerian couscous.

Every morning, our kitchen hand-rolls fine semolina for Eli’s Royal Couscous, marinates free-range poultry in Provençal herbs, sears Cypriot halloumi over open flame, and prepares our signature Tiramisu Martini for candlelit London evenings.
