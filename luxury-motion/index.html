    About LUXURY MOTION

    A Private Automotive Gallery

    LUXURY MOTION is a digital showroom built as an interactive exhibition — seven machines, one dark gallery, and a configurator that lets you dress each car your way. Drag to walk around a car, use the camera views to study its lines, and open View Details for the numbers that matter.

    An honest note on the cars: the seven vehicles here are detailed procedural 3D interpretations built in Three.js — shaped, painted and lit to each marque's identity. They are not the manufacturers' official CAD or GLB models, and no external model files are loaded. The specifications shown are the manufacturers' published figures.

    Built as a single standalone page — HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript and Three.js. No account, no backend, no tracking.
