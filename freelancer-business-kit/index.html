Questions, answered

Who is the Freelancer Business Kit for?

Freelancers and solo service providers in the USA — designers, writers, developers, marketers, consultants, virtual assistants and anyone else who sends proposals, signs clients and issues invoices. If you work project-to-project with clients, the kit was built for you.

What format are the files in?

Documents come in editable Word and Google Docs formats; the calculators and trackers are spreadsheets (Excel and Google Sheets compatible). You can change the wording, rates, colors and branding to match your business.

How do I receive the kit after buying?

It's an instant digital download. As soon as your payment is complete, you'll get access to the full set of files — no shipping, no waiting.

Is the free invoice template really free?

Yes — it's a complete, working Excel invoice template taken from the kit itself. Download it from the section above, fill in your details, and send it to your next client. No email address or payment required.

Are the contract templates legal advice?

No. They are practical, plainly written business templates based on standard freelance practice — not legal advice and not a substitute for an attorney. If a project is large or unusual, have a lawyer in your state review your contract before you sign.

Can I use the kit for more than one client?

Yes. One purchase covers your own freelance business — use the templates and trackers for as many clients and projects as you like. The files are for your business use, not for resale or redistribution.
