A private clinic that takes the time to understand you

Strides Healthcare & Artificial Limbs is a private centre in Multan for people who need a prosthetic limb, an orthotic brace, or support to move with more confidence. Our mission is simple: to empower lives, restore independence, and help you move forward.

Every limb we fit is personalised. We listen first, measure carefully, design a custom fit, and stay with you through trial fitting and rehabilitation — because comfort, function and confidence matter as much as the device itself.

Our patients tell us they felt heard, that every step was explained, and that follow-up care continued after fitting. That is the experience we work to give every person who walks through our door.

What we do

Personalised, custom-made care — not off-the-shelf solutions. Prices for devices are confirmed on consultation, so your plan is based on your own needs, measurements and goals.

Personalised prosthetics

Custom-made prosthetic limbs, including transtibial (below-knee) and above-knee prostheses — designed around accurate measurements, a custom socket, trial fitting and a fabrication process built to recognised standards.

Price confirmed on consultationEvery fit is individual. Ask us on WhatsApp for an assessment.

Custom orthotics & braces

Custom-designed braces and orthotic supports, made to support, align and stabilise — including ankle-foot orthoses (AFOs) and other custom orthotic solutions fitted to your measurements.

Price confirmed on consultationDesigned and fitted in clinic, with follow-up checks.

Cosmetic silicone hands

Lifelike, flesh-toned cosmetic silicone hands and gloves, custom-fitted for partial-hand limb difference — shaped and finished to look and feel as natural as possible.

Price confirmed on consultationSend us a message and ask about a custom fitting.

Mobility & rehabilitation

Rehabilitation and gait support to help you use your new limb with confidence — including physiotherapy support, walking practice and dedicated rehabilitation after fitting. In-person and video consultations are available.

Price confirmed on consultationRehabilitation is part of the journey, not an extra.

How it works

A clear, unhurried process — the same steps our patients describe in their reviews.

1. Consultation

We listen, understand your needs and goals, and explain what is possible — before anything is made.

2. Measurements & design

Accurate limb measurements and a custom socket design, made for your body and your daily life.

3. Trial fitting

Your device is fitted, checked and adjusted with you — comfort and function come first.

4. Rehabilitation & follow-up

Walking practice, rehabilitation support and follow-up care, so you keep moving with confidence.
