Good to know

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawal.

Price kaise decide hoti hai?

Har event alag hota hai — venue, mehmaan aur décor ke hisaab se price banti hai. Aap details bhej dein, hum WhatsApp par saaf price bata denge. Koi chhupa charge nahi.

Kitne din pehle book karna chahiye?

Jitni jaldi, utna behtar — achhi dates jaldi bhar jati hain. Kam az kam 2–3 hafte pehle rabta karein taake theme aur samaan waqt par tayyar ho.

Kya aap venue par ja kar setup karte hain?

Ji haan — Pakpattan aur aas-paas ke areas mein hum venue par poori setup karte hain, event se pehle aur event ke din team maujood rehti hai.

Apna theme ya rang bata sakte hain?

Bilkul — theme, rang aur style aap ki pasand se hoga. Apni pasand ki photos bhi bhej sakte hain, hum us hisaab se design karenge.
